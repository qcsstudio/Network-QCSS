CCNA Days 1-60 public recovery

Purpose: restore the exact already-published lessons during the Neon free-quota
outage, without publishing a draft or consuming a generation/review API.

Scope: a strict public-field bundle for all 60 published lessons, their public
metadata, teaching page, quiz, outline, course links and generated OG/social
image endpoints. Days 3, 6, 14, 22 and 24 include their applied and verified
October 21 supplements; the other lessons use the existing published snapshot.
Pending supplements for Days 2, 17 and 29 are not included. Day 1 predates the
current exact-revision review receipt and is restored solely as its already
published legacy revision; this does not create a new technical approval.
Original IDs, publication dates,
modification dates, sources and content remain unchanged. Private traces, queue
jobs, approval identities and operational errors are excluded from the bundle.

During the approved wait until 1 November 2026 00:00 UTC these 60 public pages
and the public lesson listing use the bundled revisions without a database read.
Unknown lesson slugs return 404 without contacting the suspended database during
this hold. They resume live reads after it. The full course listing and previous/
next navigation include all 60 chapters. No sitemap inventory is replaced.

After that boundary, healthy DB reads take precedence, including a null/empty
result for withdrawn content. Only recognized availability failures may serve
the last-known published recovery copies. Admin and social workers never import
this reader. No job, schedule, approval, held delivery or DB row is changed.

Withdrawal during the outage requires a code deployment removing the recovery
entry and updating its strict bundle validation/tests, or rolling back this release. Admin
draft changes cannot invalidate a disconnected bundle during the explicit hold.
Do not extend the hold automatically. Once DB access is available, verify fresh
publication state and retire the bundle if it is no longer needed. Do not use
this recovery path to approve unpublished chapters or pending supplements.

Private provenance, release checks and browser evidence belong under
test-results/ccna-full-recovery-2026-10-24; the original three-lesson evidence stays
under test-results/ccna-recovery-2026-10-24. Content hashes in the regression tests bind
the payload to the reviewed publication evidence. These tests do not claim a
GNS3, CML, hardware or networking-lab runtime test.

Read-only regression check (no database credentials or social API):
node scripts/ccna-public-health.mjs https://www.qcsstudio.com test-results/ccna-health
It checks all 60 pages, exact canonicals, indexability, section/quiz presence,
