QCS CONSULTING HOMEPAGE RELEASE
2026-09-30

Scope
Service-led homepage for India and international clients. The approved prototype
is integrated into the existing Next.js site, not deployed as a separate site.
Existing service, course, tool, content, administration and API routes remain.
No database schema, editorial automation or authentication changes.

Content and layout decisions
- Lead with network, security and cloud consulting and a project enquiry.
- Explain situations, scope, deliverables, engagement options and safeguards.
- Keep training secondary and tools a short supporting section.
- Label illustrative diagrams, example reports and scenarios honestly.
- Use real existing service destinations and the existing lead endpoint.
- Server-render headings, body copy, FAQs, links and structured data.
- Keep canonical URL and include WebPage and service ItemList structured data.
- Do not promise search rankings, AI citations, fabricated results or a universal
  100-percent readability score.

Graphics and accessibility
Six lazy Three.js scenes and scoped Canvas backgrounds. Device-pixel ratio is
capped at 2, with a pixel budget for background canvases. Motion stops offscreen,
in hidden tabs and when paused; reduced-motion preferences are respected.
Static fallbacks and crawlable text remain without the graphics enhancement.
Service tabs support keyboard navigation. All diagrams are illustrative, not
live telemetry. Resources, observers, listeners and GPU allocations are disposed
on navigation. Local Geist fonts include their upstream OFL license.
